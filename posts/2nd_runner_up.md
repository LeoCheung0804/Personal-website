<div data-lang="en">
<p>Honoured that our team won 2nd Runner Up in the Open Section of the Young Professionals Exhibition &amp; Competition 2025 with our CU-Brick Brick Laying Cable-Driven Robot for a real-world construction project. We showcased the prototype at the Purple Zone booth and delivered a technical presentation to industry leaders, demonstrating how cable-driven robotic systems can accelerate and improve safety in brick-laying tasks.</p>

<p>Grateful for the support from our teammates, advisors at CUHK, and everyone who visited our demo. Special thanks to the organisers and IET Hong Kong YMS for a well-run event, and to the industry delegates who provided valuable feedback, including representatives from EMSD, Arup, CLP, HK Electric and MTR. This recognition motivates us to continue refining the system and push towards real-world deployment in construction projects.</p>
</div>

<div data-lang="zhHant">
<p>很高興我們的團隊憑 CU-Brick 砌磚纜索驅動機械人，在 2025 年青年專業展覽及比賽公開組奪得亞軍。活動期間，我們在 Purple Zone 展位展示了原型，並向業界領袖進行技術簡報，分享纜索驅動機械人系統如何加快砌磚工序並提升安全性。</p>

<p>感謝隊友、CUHK 的導師，以及所有到場參觀示範的人。特別感謝主辦單位及 IET Hong Kong YMS 籌辦這場順利而充實的活動，也感謝來自 EMSD、Arup、CLP、HK Electric 和 MTR 等業界代表給予寶貴意見。這份肯定鼓勵我們繼續完善系統，並朝建築項目的實際部署邁進。</p>
