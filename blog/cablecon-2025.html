Conference 會議

Thrilled to have presented our paper Development of CU-Brick Brick Laying Cable-Driven Robot for a Real-World Construction Project at the 7th International Conference on Cable-Driven Parallel Robots. It was an honor to showcase how cable-driven robots are reshaping the construction industry. Grateful to the organisers and every delegate who stopped by our demo.
