CU-Brick Wins Merit Award at OSH Innovation and Technology Award 2026 CU-Brick 獲頒 2026 年 OSH 創新及科技獎嘉許獎

Competition 比賽

June 4, 2026 2026年6月4日

Honoured that CU-Brick received the Merit Award in the Open Category of the OSH Innovation and Technology Award. This recognition highlights our effort to apply cable-driven robotics to improve safety and productivity in brick-laying tasks.

The OSH Innovation and Technology Award encourages the public and students to unleash their creativity by submitting innovative designs, projects, or ideas that improve workplace safety and health. It supports the use of technology to solve safety and health problems at work, helping industries reduce accidents and build better working environments. The award also invites schools and companies to develop new safety products and technologies, strengthening OSH development in Hong Kong.

Grateful to the organisers and everyone who supported the project. This award motivates us to keep refining CU-Brick and move closer to real-world deployment on construction sites.

很高興 CU-Brick 在 職安健創科大獎 公開組獲得嘉許獎。這份肯定反映了我們把纜索驅動機械人應用於砌磚工序，從而提升安全和效率的努力。

職安健創科大獎鼓勵公眾和學生提交能改善職安健的創新設計、項目或構想，推動以科技解決工作場所的安全和健康問題，協助業界減少意外並建立更好的工作環境。獎項同時鼓勵學校與企業開發新的安全產品和技術，進一步推動香港的職安健發展。

感謝主辦單位以及一直支持這個項目的人。這個獎項鼓勵我們繼續完善 CU-Brick，並向建築工地的實際應用再邁進一步。
