Young Professionals Exhibition & Competition 2025 參與 YPEC 2025

Competition 比賽

November 2, 2025 2025年11月2日

Proud to have participated in the Young Professionals Exhibition & Competition 2025 at City University of Hong Kong. We showcased CU-Brick at the Purple Zone booth, delivered a technical presentation, and exchanged ideas with representatives from EMSD, Arup, CLP, HK Electric, and MTR. Grateful for the opportunity to represent CUHK and connect with fellow engineers. Special thanks to IET Hong Kong YMS for organising the event.

很高興在香港城市大學參加 YPEC 2025。我們在 Purple Zone 展位展示 CU-Brick，並向業界代表進行簡報，亦與來自機電工程署、Arup、CLP、HK Electric 和 MTR 的代表交流。很感恩能代表 CUHK 與其他工程師建立聯繫，亦特別感謝 IET Hong Kong YMS 舉辦這場充實的活動。
