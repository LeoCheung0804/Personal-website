Young Professionals Exhibition & Competition 2025 2025 年青年專業展覽及比賽

Competition 比賽

November 2, 2025 2025年11月2日

Proud to have participated in the Young Professionals Exhibition & Competition 2025 today at City University of Hong Kong. We showcased our project at the Purple Zone booth and delivered a presentation to industry leaders. We also met with the Director of EMSD and executives from Arup, CLP, HK Electric, and MTR. Grateful for the opportunity to represent CUHK and connect with fellow engineers. Special thanks to IET Hong Kong YMS for this inspiring event!

今天很高興在香港城市大學參加 2025 年 青年科技專才展覽及比賽。我們在 Purple Zone 展位展示了項目，並向業界領袖進行簡報，亦與機電工程署署長以及來自 Arup、CLP、HK Electric 和 MTR 的管理層交流。很感恩能代表 CUHK 與其他工程師建立聯繫，亦特別感謝 IET Hong Kong YMS 舉辦這場啟發性的活動。
