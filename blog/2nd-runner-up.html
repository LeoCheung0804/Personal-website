CU-Brick Named Second Runner-up at YPEC 2025 CU-Brick 榮獲 YPEC 2025 公開組季軍

Competition 比賽

November 2, 2025 2025年11月2日

Honoured that CU-Brick was named second runner-up in the Open Section at the Young Professionals Exhibition & Competition 2025. We showcased the cable-driven bricklaying robot at the Purple Zone booth and delivered a technical presentation to industry representatives, demonstrating how the system can improve productivity and safety in real construction work.

Grateful for the support from our teammates, advisors at CUHK, and everyone who visited our demo. Special thanks to the organisers and IET Hong Kong YMS for a well-run event, and to the industry delegates who provided valuable feedback, including representatives from EMSD, Arup, CLP, HK Electric and MTR. This recognition motivates us to continue refining the system and push towards real-world deployment in construction projects.

很高興 CU-Brick 在 YPEC 2025 公開組獲得季軍。活動期間，我們在 Purple Zone 展位展示纜索驅動砌磚機械人，並向業界代表進行技術簡報，分享系統如何在實際建造工作中提升效率與安全。

感謝隊友、CUHK 的導師，以及所有到場參觀示範的人。特別感謝主辦單位及 IET Hong Kong YMS 籌辦這場順利而充實的活動，也感謝來自 EMSD、Arup、CLP、HK Electric 和 MTR 等業界代表給予寶貴意見。這份肯定鼓勵我們繼續完善系統，並朝建築項目的實際部署邁進。
